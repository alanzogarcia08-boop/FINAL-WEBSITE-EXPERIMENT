About The Real Estate Broker

Ma. Cecilia Fermina R. Dela Cruz is a Licensed Real Estate Broker serving Pampanga since 2013 and the founder of NiCeity Realty. She has helped countless families, OFWs, first-time homebuyers, and investors achieve their homeownership and investment goals through honest guidance, professional service and personalized support.

Specializing in mid-range to luxury homes, she offers a carefully selected portfolio of ready-for-occupancy (RFO) homes and pre-selling residential projects in Pampanga, helping clients find the property that best fits their lifestyle and investment goals.

Known for her integrity, heart-centered approach, excellent customer service and commitment to her clients, she is dedicated to making every real estate journey smooth, informed, and rewarding.

Backed by genuine reviews from happy and satisfied clients, Ma. Cecilia continues to earn the trust of those looking for a reliable real estate professional who will guide them every step of the way.
